# A Small Cloud

*A little girl who cannot sleep befriends the smallest cloud in the sky.*

## The poem underneath

> The night is very large, and the cloud is very small.
> It drifts past her window, alone, the way she feels in the dark.
> She turns off her light so it can see her better —
> and the room becomes sky.
> In the dream there is nothing under her feet but weather.
> The small cloud is shy. Then it is a rabbit. Then it is a whale,
> and she rides it to the edge of morning.
> Clouds cannot hold hands, so it rains, very gently, on her palm.
> When she wakes, it is waiting at the window.

**The one colour:** a warm amber — the colour of comfort. It is the nightlight she switches off, the
dawn sun in the dream, the glow of the cloud when it becomes her friend, and the cloud's tint in the
morning. It leaves the film when she turns off the lamp, and returns as friendship.

**Characters**
- *The girl* — about seven: big head, short bob, a long nightgown, bare feet. She moves carefully, then freely.
- *The small cloud* — no face. All character is motion: drifting, hesitating, peeking, bouncing,
  stretching. It is the size of a dog; it grows only to carry her.

## Shot plan (≈ 90 s)

1. **Window** — the window from inside: stars, a crescent moon, a small cloud crossing alone. Title.
2. **Room** — wide: she sits up in bed by the window; the lamp glows amber.
3. **Watching** — her profile against the moonlit pane; her head follows the cloud.
4. **The cloud stops** — POV through the glass: it pauses by the moon, as if it noticed her.
5. **Lamp** — she reaches out and switches the lamp off; she sinks under the blanket.
6. **Into the window** — the camera pushes into the pane until its light is all there is.
7. **Cloud sea** — dream: she stands small on a sea of cloud lines that draws itself out from her feet.
8. **Walking** — profile, left to right, barefoot on weather.
9. **Peek** — a tall tower; the small cloud peeks out from behind it, and hides.
10. **Hand** — close: she holds out her hand.
11. **Shy** — it comes close, backs away, comes back, circles her.
12. **Rabbit** — it becomes a rabbit and hops; she hops too.
13. **Whale** — it grows into a whale and lowers itself; she climbs on.
14. **Flight** — tracking alongside: she rides it low over the cloud sea; towers pass.
15. **Dawn** — high and wide: the horizon warms; the sun — the amber — rises.
16. **Goodbye** — they land; it shrinks to its small self, glows, and rains gently on her hand.
17. **Morning** — her room in daylight; at the window, the small cloud waiting, tinted with dawn.
18. **End card** — *and every night after, it waited by her window.*
