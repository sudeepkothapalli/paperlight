# The Bridge

**Central idea** — On a bridge too narrow for two, impatience turns into kindness the moment something small is about to be lost.

**Logline** — A brisk man and a skipping girl meet at the top of a narrow footbridge at dusk. They block each other — twice, in step. A gust takes her balloon; he drops his case, jumps, catches it, and kneels to give it back. They pass, and the balloon becomes the moon.

**Register** — comic, then tender. Not melancholy. Quick cuts on the music for the comedy; a held two-shot for the kindness.

**Emotional arc** — `ease → annoyance (comic mirror) → alarm → kindness → warmth`
