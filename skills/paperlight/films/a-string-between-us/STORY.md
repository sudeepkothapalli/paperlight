# A String Between Us

**Brief (as given):** two silhouette characters separated by an abstract flowing landscape, a red kite carried by
the wind, a shared thread, and a gradual emotional transition from isolation to connection.

**Central idea** — The thing you send out alone can come back to you with someone holding the other end.

**Logline** — Every evening an old man flies a red kite on his side of a wide, flowing land; across it, a young woman
sits alone on her doorstep. A gust carries the kite all the way to her. She picks it up — and the string still runs
back to his hand. She tugs once. He waves. She sends the kite up again, and they walk toward each other along the
line until there is no distance left.

**Emotional arc** — `isolation → noticing → loss of control (the gust) → a gift arrives → a question (one tug) → an
answer (a wave) → shared joy (the relaunch) → approach → together`

**The poem underneath**
> He flew it for nobody, every evening.
> The wind decided otherwise.
> She found it at her door, still tied to him.
> One pull. One wave.
> Then the long walk, winding the distance in.

## Wordless conversation
The device: **the thread** — one string, two hands, a pull that travels.
| # | who | the move | what it means | how it arrives | the reply |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | the wind | carries his kite to her door | (chance) | it lands by her | she picks it up |
| 2 | Mara | one tug | *are you there?* | a ripple runs the whole line and jumps in his hand | he looks at his hand, then across |
| 3 | Ivo | lifts his free hand and waves | *I am. Hello.* | she sees the small figure wave | she laughs, a hop |
| 4 | Mara | sends the kite up again | *let's fly it together* | the string lifts off the land between them | they walk toward each other, winding in |
